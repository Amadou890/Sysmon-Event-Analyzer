Sysmon Event Analyzer

Paste a Sysmon or Windows event and get a plain-English triage, the matching MITRE ATT&CK technique, and a Splunk query to pivot on. Companion to the Splunk Threat Hunting Lab.

Rule-based triage aid. It runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Sample events are synthetic. Covers Sysmon Event IDs 1 (process creation), 3 (network connection), 13 (registry value set), and the Windows log-cleared events 1102 and 104.

Limits: pattern matching, not a verdict. A match is a lead to verify with parent processes, user context, and timing. No match does not mean an event is safe. Thresholds and patterns are starting points to tune against your own baseline.
